# Approche 9 : Company Partnership — Co-Marketing Gratuit

> Partenaire avec grandes marques gaming (GPU makers, console makers, gaming companies).
> Eux fournissent les lots gratuitement (marketing pour eux), vous lancez Op1 avec leur
> endorsement (validation pour vous).

---

## Concept Core

**Win-win partnership** :

```text
Leur besoin:
├─ Brand exposure
├─ Community engagement
├─ PR story: "transparence + gaming community"
└─ Cost: lots they already manufacture

Votre besoin:
├─ Crédibilité (big brand backing)
├─ Lots (gratuit instead 1-2k EUR)
├─ Amplification (they share Op1 to their audience)
└─ Cost: ~zéro EUR
```

**Result:** Op1 with 3-5 big brands, zero lot cost, maximum credibility.

### Phase 3 : Outreach & Negotiation (Semaines -3 à -1)

#### Outreach Strategy

```text
Wave 1 (Best targets first):
├─ Identify 3-5 top companies
├─ Personalized email (mention recent campaign, show you know them)
├─ Use mutual LinkedIn connections if possible
├─ Wait 3 days, follow up if no response

Wave 2 (Secondary targets):
├─ 5-10 additional companies
├─ Same approach
├─ Slightly broader, less personalized
├─ More chance of "yes" (less selective)

Timing:
├─ Send emails 6 weeks before Op1
├─ Most companies respond within 2 weeks
├─ Negotiation timeline: 2-3 weeks
├─ Final confirmation: 2 weeks before Op1 launch

Success rate expectation:
├─ Outreach: 20-30 companies
├─ Responses: 20% (6 responses)
├─ Negotiations: 50% of responses agree (3 yes)
├─ Confirmed: 3-5 partners
```

#### Negotiation Points

```text
If they say "maybe," address concerns:

Concern: "Is this a scam yourself?"
└─ Response: Send legal docs (statuts, SIREN, huissier contract, avocat letter)

Concern: "Will this reflect poorly on us?"
└─ Response: Share case study of how transparent this is
└─ Mention: media coverage, legal backing, community validation

Concern: "What's the catch?"
└─ Response: "No catch. We benefit from partnership. You get PR."
└─ Honest about: "We're building credibility for grassroots initiative"

Concern: "What if something goes wrong?"
└─ Response: "Worst case, you get good PR ('company supported transparency')"
└─ "Best case, partner with growing platform"

Negotiation:
├─ You ask: 1-3 products
├─ They counter: "Only 1 product" → Accept
├─ You ask: Public mention
├─ They counter: "Mention okay, logo maybe not" → Accept
├─ Flexibility wins deals
```

## Avantages Clés

✅ **Zero lot cost**

- Companies provide products (marketing donation)
- Save 1-2k EUR on lots
- Can reinvest elsewhere

✅ **Maximum credibility**

- "Backed by NVIDIA" > anything else
- Big brand = third-party validation
- Impossible to fake

✅ **Amplification**

- Partners have huge audiences
- One retweet = 100k impressions
- Op1 gets 10x more participants

✅ **PR story**

- "Startup partners with major brands"
- Journalists love this angle
- Media coverage potential

✅ **Relationship building**

- Start relationships with big companies
- Potential for ongoing partnerships
- Moat: hard for competitors to replicate

## Risques & Mitigations

| Risque | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Companies say no | Have 20+ targets, expect 20-30% conversion |
| Products late | Start outreach 8 weeks before Op1, not 4 |
| Company wants something in return | Negotiate clearly upfront |
| Company doesn't amplify | They agreed to partnership, polite reminder works |
| Perception: "bought validation" | Frame as mutual benefit, not bought |

## Recommendation

**Best for :**

- Want maximum credibility (big brand backing)
- Want zero lot cost (companies provide)
- Can handle negotiation / relationship building
- 8-10 week timeline acceptable
- Like working with established companies

**Not ideal for :**

- Need Op1 in <6 weeks (outreach takes time)
- Don't like sales/negotiation process
- Want to avoid "corporate" positioning
- Small budget timelines

## ⚠️ Important: Clarity on Partnership

**This approach does NOT require payment.** It's mutualistic:

```text
You don't pay companies for products.
Companies don't pay you for partnership.

Exchange:
├─ You: Provide platform to showcase company products
├─ Company: Provides products (marketing donation)

Both benefit:
├─ You: Get credibility + zero product cost
├─ Company: Get PR + community goodwill

It's a gift exchange, not a business deal.
```
