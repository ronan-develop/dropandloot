# Build in Public — Stratégie Zero Budget

> Construire Drop & Loot en public, transparence radicale, authenticity maximale.
> Pour teams sans budget initial, mais avec motivation + idées.

---

## Executive Summary

**Problème** : Zéro audience, zéro budget, zéro enseigne. Comment créer crédibilité?

**Solution** : Build in public.

Documentez TOUT (erreurs incluses). Laissez la communauté voir le chaos et comment vous le
fixez. **Ça crée confiance** — pas via contrats ou paroles polies, mais via transparence
radicale.

**Avantages** :
- Coût : 0 EUR (juste du temps)
- Crédibilité : +300% (authenticity > polish)
- Community : Self-selected (seulement intéressés par transparence)
- Moat : Impossible à copier (autres startups ont budget, vous avez honesty)

---

## 🎯 Core Philosophy

**Ne pas** : "Lancer Op1 parfait, puis dire aux gens"
**Faire** : "Construire Op1 EN PUBLIC, gens regardent et valident en temps réel"

```
Traditional Startup:
├─ Mois 1-3 : Build en secret
├─ Mois 4 : Launch avec boom
├─ Résultat : Hope people believe you

Build in Public:
├─ Jour 1 : Announce on Twitter/Discord
├─ Jours 2-14 : Show every decision (good + bad)
├─ Jour 15 : People already know you, they want you to succeed
├─ Launch : Organic (no boom needed, momentum exists)
```

**Why it works for YOU** :
- Gaming community appreciates realness
- Showing vulnerability = credibility (vs fake polish)
- Early audience = built-in validators
- "I watched them build this" = I trust them

**Thread 2 : Day 3 — "Legality Call with Lawyer"**

```
🧵 Day 3 update : Called avocat. Here's what we learned 
                  (and what we got wrong).

What we thought:
├─ "Giveaways = simple, just random draw"

Reality:
├─ France has specific law: L.121-20 (Code de la consommation)
│  + L.320-1 CSI (paid entry to a draw = prohibited lottery)
├─ Giveaways must be: free to enter (no extra cost), fair, published rules,
                       proof of draw
├─ Huissier (commissaire de justice): recommended, not mandatory since 2014

Cost impact:
├─ Avocat: 300-500 EUR (one-time legal review) ← more than we budgeted!
├─ Huissier: 400-600 EUR per operation ← expected but still ouch

Positives:
├─ It's legal (confirmed)
├─ Protections are real (not just marketing)
├─ Not complicated (if done right)

Next:
├─ [ ] Call 3 huissiers for actual pricing + contracts
├─ [ ] Understand exact L.121-20 C. conso + L.320-1 CSI requirements
├─ [ ] Revise RGPD policy with lawyer input

Questions? What did we miss?
```

## ⚠️ Risk Mitigation (No Budget)

**Risk 1 : Influencer says "this is sus"**
```
├─ You already knew it was possible
├─ You said "honest feedback only"
├─ You listen, iterate, try again
├─ Or pivot to different influencer
├─ Cost = learning, not credibility death
```

**Risk 2 : Lot doesn't arrive in time**
```
├─ Post immediately on Discord/Twitter
├─ "We hit a delay [reason]. Honest communication: [new timeline]"
├─ Offer bonus (if possible) or rescheduled Op1
├─ Community respects honesty > false promises
```

**Risk 3 : Zero followers, zero engagement**
```
├─ Real possibility with zero budget
├─ But: you still built real credibility (docs, process, honesty)
├─ Use Op1 as proof point for Op2+ outreach
├─ "Look, we actually did this" > "we promise we will"
```

**Risk 4 : Gagnant doesn't want to go public**
```
├─ Respect their privacy
├─ Share only numbers ("1 gagnant received lot, happy")
├─ Testimonial = optional, not required
├─ Still proves it wasn't fake
```

## 🚀 Why This Works (Psychology)

**People believe you because:**

1. **Transparency is costly** (you're showing vulnerabilities)
   - Easy to fake polish
   - Hard to fake 2-week journey with documented mistakes

2. **You're asking for help** (not selling)
   - Humans help underdogs
   - "Build in public" = you need community
   - Community becomes invested ("I helped build this")

3. **No hidden agenda** (you're broke, not manipulating)
   - Free model = no incentive to scam
   - "They're doing this for real"

4. **Real-time documentation** (can't fake)
   - Screenshots of lawyer emails = real
   - Photos of documents = real
   - Daily updates = accountable

5. **Early adopters are validators**
   - Not hired testimonials
   - People who chose to watch your journey
   - They WANT you to win
   - They'll defend you
