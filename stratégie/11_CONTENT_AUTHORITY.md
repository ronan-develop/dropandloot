# Approche 7 : Content Authority — Éducation → Produit

> Devenir l'autorité incontournable sur "comment reconnaître et faire un bon giveaway",
> puis présenter Drop & Loot comme solution naturelle.

---

## Concept Core

**Build audience + authority via content éducatif**, puis revealer Drop & Loot comme
l'outil que audience demande depuis le début.

Plus large que Trojan Horse (pas juste expertise personnelle, mais vrai content business).

### Différence avec Trojan Horse

```
Trojan Horse:
├─ Vous êtes l'expert
├─ Révélation = perso, c'est VOUS qui avez construit
├─ Audience suits YOU

Content Authority:
├─ Vous bâtissez RESOURCE/GUIDE (pas juste YOU)
├─ Révélation = voici l'outil pour le guide
├─ Audience suits la RESSOURCE
├─ Plus durable, plus scalable
```

## Phases d'Exécution

### Phase 1 : Content Pillar (Semaines -12 à -8)

#### Concept : "The Complete Guide to Not Getting Scammed by Giveaways"

```
Vaste ressource couvrant:

1. Types de scams
   ├─ Fake lots
   ├─ Data harvesting
   ├─ Non-livraison
   └─ Rigged draws

2. How to spot red flags
   ├─ 20-point checklist
   ├─ Real examples
   ├─ What legitimate looks like

3. How giveaways should work
   ├─ Legal framework (France)
   ├─ Transparency markers
   ├─ What to demand from organizers

4. For organizers: How to do it right
   ├─ Legal requirements
   ├─ Best practices
   ├─ Infrastructure needed

5. Tools & resources
   ├─ Where to report scams
   ├─ Who to contact
   ├─ Documentation templates
```

#### Format Multi-Platform

```
Content hub (website, Medium, Substack):
├─ Main guide (long-form, 5000+ words, evergreen)
├─ Downloadable resources (checklist, templates)
├─ Links to external authorities (CNIL, legal sites)
├─ Email signup (build list for later)

YouTube:
├─ 5-10 video series (each aspect of guide)
├─ Explainer videos (30 min total)
├─ Case studies (real scams analyzed)

Twitter/Reddit:
├─ Thread series (snapshots of guide)
├─ Daily tips
├─ Crowdsource : ask audience about their scam stories

Podcast (optional):
├─ Interview experts (avocats, huissiers, streamers)
├─ Deep-dive episodes
├─ Build authority via audio
```

#### Production Timeline

```
Semaine -12 : Framework + research
├─ Outline complete guide
├─ Research: real cases, legal framework, expert quotes
├─ Plan 10 video topics

Semaine -11 : Content creation burst
├─ Write main guide (draft)
├─ Film 3-4 videos
├─ Prepare Twitter series

Semaines -10 à -8 : Publishing + amplification
├─ Publish main guide
├─ Release videos on schedule (1-2/week)
├─ Twitter: daily thread
├─ Gather feedback, iterate
```

### Phase 2 : Authority Buildup (Semaines -7 à -4)

#### Distribution Strategy

```
Where to publish:

1. Reddit communities
   └─ r/gaming, r/france, r/scams, subreddits spécialisés
   └─ Share guide (not self-promote, add value)
   └─ Answer questions as "guide author"
   └─ Result: backlinks + credibility

2. Gaming blogs / news sites
   └─ Contact site owners: "Can I guest post on giveaway scams?"
   └─ Provide article + guide excerpt
   └─ Result: backlinks, authority, reach

3. Discord communities
   └─ Find gaming Discords with #scams or #giveaways channels
   └─ Share guide in relevant places (not spam)
   └─ Become known as "that person who knows giveaways"

4. Twitter / X
   └─ Thread series
   └─ Engage with gaming discourse
   └─ Retweet relevant discussions + add your expertise

5. Podcast appearances (if possible)
   └─ Pitch yourself to gaming podcasts
   └─ "Expert on giveaway safety" angle
   └─ Mention guide in bio
```

#### Engagement Phase

```
Key is to NOT sell anything yet. Just help.

Example interactions:

Reddit thread: "Got scammed by a giveaway, lost my email to spammers"
├─ Your response: "I made a guide on this exact thing [link], sorry this happened"
├─ Not: "Buy my service!"
├─ Effect: helpful, credible, unobtrusive

Twitter: "Why are giveaways so sus?"
├─ Your thread: "5 reasons [link to guide], here's what legitimate looks like"
├─ Not: "Download my app!"
├─ Effect: expert, helpful, visible

Discord: Someone asking "Is this giveaway safe?"
├─ You help with your 20-point checklist
├─ Link guide for deeper analysis
├─ Not: mention product
├─ Effect: trusted advisor
```

- Guide = evergreen, continues helping people

- Only people who care about giveaway safety

- Pre-educated for solution

- Can monetize guide (premium version)
- Can add affiliate products later

- Hard to replicate (requires real expertise + content volume)

## Risques & Mitigations

| Risque | Mitigation |
|--------|-----------|
| Guide takes very long (18 weeks total) | Start with 80% MVP guide, iterate |
| Audience slow to grow | Consistency > viral. 1000 engaged > 100k distracted |
| Reveal falls flat | Over-deliver on Op1, guide → product is natural step |
| Audience wants free forever | Offer free guide, paid services (Drop & Loot) later |
