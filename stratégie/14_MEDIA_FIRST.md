# Approche 10 : Media First — PR Avant Lancement

> Contacter journalistes/médias AVANT lancement publique.
> Obtenir article de presse, PUIS lancer Op1 (presse = third-party validation jour 1).

---

## Concept Core

**Press before product.**

Presse = ultimate credibility. Si journaliste renommé parle de vous, c'est validation.

```
Traditional approach:
├─ You launch product
├─ Hope media covers
├─ Slow traction

Media First:
├─ You pitch journalists (off-record)
├─ Journalists validate, write article
├─ Article publié 1 jour avant launch
├─ Public launch = "As seen in [Media Major]"
├─ Boom: instant credibility + reach
```

### Phase 3 : Journalist Engagement & Interviews (Semaines -4 à -2)

#### If Journalist Responds

```
Typical response: "Interesting. Tell me more."

Next step: Setup call or email followup

---

Call agenda (30 min):

00:00-05:00 | Journalist background
├─ Ask about their recent coverage
├─ Show you know their work
├─ Build rapport

05:00-10:00 | Problem statement
├─ Paint picture: gaming giveaway market is broken
├─ Statistics: scams, data theft, non-delivery
├─ Why now: market awareness is high

10:00-15:00 | Your solution
├─ What Drop & Loot is
├─ Legal framework (huissier, L.121-36)
├─ Transparency model
├─ Op1 timeline

15:00-20:00 | Interview logistics
├─ Can they visit? (see operation in real-time)
├─ Can they interview founders?
├─ Exclusive timing? (do they want exclusive?)
├─ Publication timeline (when would article run?)

20:00-30:00 | Q&A
├─ Address questions / concerns
├─ Offer access to documents
├─ Next steps

---

After call: send documentation

├─ Legal docs (SIREN, statuts, huissier contract)
├─ RGPD certification
├─ Team bios (LinkedIn, backgrounds)
├─ Op1 logistics (lot details, timeline, proof)
├─ Case study (if Op0 test happened)
```

#### Transparency with Journalist

```
Important: be honest

✓ "We're a startup, just launching"
✓ "We have zero audience right now"
✓ "We're building credibility from scratch"
✓ "Our founders are two people, no VCs yet"

This is BETTER than:

✗ Exaggerating scale
✗ Claiming experience we don't have
✗ Hiding that we're new

Journalists appreciate honesty. 
"Scrappy startup doing something right" > "fake it till you make it"
```

#### Interview Logistics

```
If journalist wants to observe Op1:

Option 1: Virtual (easiest)
├─ Zoom call during tirage
├─ Show everything on screen
├─ Huissier on camera
├─ Real-time proof

Option 2: In-person (better for story)
├─ Journalist visits co-working day of tirage
├─ Sees everything live
├─ Photos/video
├─ Much more immersive

Option 3: Hybrid
├─ Meet in person before tirage
├─ Interview founders
├─ Join virtual tirage
├─ Best of both

What journalist likely needs:

├─ Founder photos (for article)
├─ Quotes (recorded + written)
├─ Video of tirage (for article/social)
├─ Numbers (participants, prize value)
├─ Timeline (confirmed dates)
```

### Phase 4 : Article Publication & Launch (Semaine -1 à 0)

#### Publication Timing

```
Ideal timeline:

├─ Confirm publication date (should be 2-3 days before Op1)
├─ Confirm angle / messaging

Semaine -1:
├─ Final interview / fact-checking

Jour -1: Article published
├─ "Drop & Loot launches with [description]"

├─ Announce: "As covered by [Media]"
```

## Avantages Clés

✅ **Third-party validation**
- Journalist vets your story
- Publication = credibility transfer
- Impossible to fake

✅ **Amplified reach**
- Major outlet = 10k-100k+ readers
- Op1 launch not cold (audience primed)
- Organic reach impossible otherwise

✅ **Professional positioning**
- "Featured in [Major Media]" on your website
- Signals: professional, credible, real
- Future journalists more likely to cover (existing press)

✅ **Continuing credibility**
- Relationship with journalist = ongoing coverage
- Op2+ gets easier to pitch
- Press coverage snowball effect

✅ **Story angle**
- Journalist writes YOUR narrative
- Better than you trying to write it yourself
- Reaches audiences who trust media

## Risques & Mitigations

| Risque | Mitigation |
|--------|-----------|
| Journalists say no | Have 5-10 targets, expect 20% response rate |
| Article doesn't run | Follow up, offer exclusivity, adjust angle |
| Article is critical | Better honest coverage than no coverage |
| Timing slips | Confirm publication date 2 weeks before Op1 |
| Op1 problems = bad PR | Have mitigation plans (see doc 06) |

## Recommendation

**Best for :**
- Have good story angle (not just "another giveaway")
- Comfortable with journalist scrutiny
- Can handle media relations / interviews
- Want maximum reach + credibility
- 14-week timeline acceptable

**Not ideal for :**
- Need Op1 in <6 weeks (press takes time)
- Want to stay under the radar (media = visibility)
- Don't like public speaking / interviews
- Worried about negative coverage (journalists are critical)

---

## ⚠️ Key Point: Honesty with Press

**Be truthful from day 1.**

Journalists will fact-check. If you:
- Exaggerate numbers ❌
- Fake credentials ❌
- Hide being a startup ❌

They'll find out, and article gets spiked or becomes negative.

Instead:

- "We're new, but we've solved a real problem" ✓
- "Our founders have X years experience in Y" ✓
- "We're backed by real legal infrastructure" ✓

Honesty + substance = great article.
